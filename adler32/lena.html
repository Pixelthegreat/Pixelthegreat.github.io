Lena is a young radio host for 111.1 AdlerFM, coming from Elaine county with a degree in Radio Technology. However, her charming personality and quick wit have garnered great support from the listeners of 111.1 AdlerFM. You can hear Lena present on Mondays through Fridays, at 1 to 5 PM.
