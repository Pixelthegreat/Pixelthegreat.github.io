## Big Fat Jones: Legacy

&emsp;"Ssh!" Jeremy warned. "Here comes Mr. Garrison!"

&emsp;Stupid Garrison was the school's superintendent and P.E. teacher. As you could probably tell by the name, he was quite stupid.

&emsp;"Well hello kids!" He had a basketball clutched in his left arm and was nervously tapping his fingers. "I hope you're all following school rules!"

&emsp;Although Xavier wished to speak up, he was caught off guard by Big Fat Jones, who had a very wide grin on his face and a butcher knife firm in his grip.

&emsp;"Yes, we are following school rules!" Jeremy said, noticing Big as well.

&emsp;"No kids by the name of Big Fat Jones, I presume?"

&emsp;"Not a soul!" Jeremy quickly replied.

&emsp;"Oh, ho ho! I'm just joking. Of course you guys wouldn't be harboring fugitives! That would be silly."

&emsp;Just then, everyone heard footsteps behind them. Stupid turned around to see that it was none other than the school principal, Mr. Grefourous Poophead Gandle.

&emsp;"I've gotta run," Stupid warned. He then ran towards one of the two basketball hoops and failed miserably at trying to throw the ball into it.

&emsp;"Stupid H. Garrison," Grefourous greeted. As he walked to the superintendent, he held a bag of cocoa beans and slowly ate them one by one. To finish, he then took an ice cream scoop and ate a scoopful of flour from a bag that he had taped to his behind.

&emsp;"I'm warning you," Grefourous said in a menacing tone, "you can either go the way that you are going now, or you can take a different path. A path where you will be set free from your burdens!"

&emsp;"Two plus two equals five," Stupid said, struggling to find words to speak.

&emsp;"When your done making your decision, you can come join us at the Raw Meat Banquet."

&emsp;Grefourous took one final look at Stupid before leaving his vicinity. The look was almost sorrowful, as if Stupid had done something awful that could've determined the fate of the universe.

## Epilogue

&emsp;Five days. It almost seems ridiculous when you put it like that. It was always just a blip, and yet, a lifetime. Five days. I laughed out loud. I got some weird looks. Five weeks. The cold and unforgiving shadow of winter loomed above the town. It wasn't there before.

&emsp;Four weeks ago, I asked if I could see her. They said no. I asked _them_ if I could see her. They said no. Something about safety. Let fate be the judge of that.

&emsp;Everyone moved on, made her the next Gatsby. I didn't. There was one good spark in the world. A little firefly trapped and locked away in my mind.

&emsp;One day, I sat on the steps, watching the cars. I never noticed them before. They all passed by in a rush of afternoon fervor. Occasionally, things appeared behind them. One car revealed it and the next hid it away again. But one thing appeared that didn't leave sight. I stood up and walked slowly over. It was a person, standing across the road. I made my way further to the curb. The cars disappeared. It was Jenna. I quietly waved. She waved back.
