# Awaken, Part 4
_by Elliot Kohlmyer, October 29th, 2026_

&emsp;_Awaken, Part 4: Berring Stone_ can now be read [here](writing.html?page=fiction/awaken/index). Well, it's finally over and I can move on. In all honesty I really did enjoy writing this, but I made a couple of mistakes with this one:
- I made hard deadlines too early
- I wrote _and_ released it in parts

&emsp;As such, I don't think it ended up as good as it could've. Nevertheless, I can't complain that I actually finished a story for the first time in something like five years. Yeesh.

&emsp;Now that the story has concluded, I decided to include some extras as well. I think I will make it a common practice for any longer or multipart stories in the future to have some extra goodies at the end, I have so much fun making them.

&emsp;As always, check up on this blog every now and then for stuff. I hope to post more regularly now that Awaken doesn't have to take up so much space in my mind.
